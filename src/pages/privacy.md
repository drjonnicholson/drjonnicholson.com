## Who I Am

This website is operated by Jon Nicholson and is intended to showcase professional experience, publications, projects, and related content.

## Analytics

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are visited and how people navigate between pages.

Google Analytics cookies are only set after you have given consent through the cookie banner. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the Cookie Settings link available on this website.

Analytics data may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

## Cookies

This website uses one cookie to remember your cookie preferences and, if you choose to enable analytics, Google Analytics cookies that help me understand how the site is used.

### Cookie preferences

- `cc_cookie` - stores your consent preferences.

### Analytics (optional)

- `_ga`
- `_ga_*`

These cookies are set by Google Analytics only after you have given consent.

## Information You Provide

If you contact me directly by email or through linked social platforms, any information you provide will only be used to respond to your enquiry.

## Your Choices

You can:

- Change your cookie preferences at any time using the Cookie Settings link.
- Clear cookies using your browser settings.
